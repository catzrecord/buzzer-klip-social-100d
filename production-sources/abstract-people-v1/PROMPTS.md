# Abstract Human Source Set V1

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool. Every source used the five files in
`previews/pusatklip-abstract-carousel-a/` as style, lighting, framing, material,
and color references only.

Shared direction:

- 4:5 cinematic Instagram artwork
- deep black background
- anonymous faceless adult figures
- glossy black glass and translucent crystal materials
- lime, cyan, magenta, and lavender neon rim light
- circular podium, volumetric smoke, sparks, and restrained shards
- calm top corners and dark lower area for deterministic typography
- artwork only: no text, logo, watermark, phone, realistic skin, or identity

Subjects:

1. Figure holding an acid-lime crystal inside a neon halo.
2. Bust with geometric thought fragments emerging from the head.
3. Dynamic runner made from black-glass ribbons and crystal segments.
4. Conductor/editor arranging floating clip panels.
5. Creator walking toward a layered neon portal.
6. Profile bust with a luminous waveform inside the head.
7. Seated thinker holding a glowing orb.
8. Two creators passing a luminous shard.
9. Creator reaching toward floating clip tiles.
10. Kneeling creator assembling a chain of clip tiles.
11. Walking creator carrying a large luminous ring.
12. Floating meditative creator inside an audio-wave halo.

The final 272 feed assets are rendered by
`scripts/generate_abstract_human_assets.py`, which rotates these sources,
applies deterministic framing and atmosphere variants, then composites the
transparent Buzzer Klip PNG and editorial copy.
